Privacy Policy

Impro It · Last updated: 15 November 2026

Impro It is a small team in Uzbekistan that builds websites, Telegram bots and promotional videos. This policy explains how our Instagram assistant handles your information when you send a direct message to our Instagram account.

What we receive

  • The text of the messages you send to our Instagram account.
  • Your public Instagram profile name and username, and your Instagram-scoped ID.
  • Contact details you choose to write in the chat, such as your name or phone number.

We do not receive your password, your followers, your posts or messages you send to other accounts.

How we use it

  • To reply to your message. Replies may be written by an automated AI assistant or by a member of our team.
  • To understand what service you need and to contact you about your request.
  • To count daily enquiries for our own internal reports.

We do not use your messages for advertising, and we do not sell your information.

Who processes it

  • Meta (Instagram) delivers your messages to us through the Instagram API.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database, and runs the AI model that drafts replies.
  • Telegram is used to notify our team about new enquiries.

We share your information with nobody else, unless the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep the conversation and your contact details while your enquiry is active and for up to 12 months afterwards, then delete them. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

How to delete your data

Send the word DELETE in a direct message to our Instagram account, or ask us there in your own words. We will delete your conversation and contact details from our systems within 7 days and confirm it in the chat. You can also ask for a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to correct it.

Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date. For any privacy question, write to us in Instagram Direct or call +998 33 623 33 13.
